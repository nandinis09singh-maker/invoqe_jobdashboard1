About the role

Northstar Labs is looking for a Product Designer to join our Bengaluru team and help shape intuitive experiences for teams using our AI-powered collaboration platform. You'll collaborate with product managers, engineers, and researchers to turn customer needs into clear product direction, polished flows, and measurable improvements.

This role is ideal for a recent graduate or early-career designer looking to build a strong foundation in product design. You'll learn from experienced designers, contribute to real product decisions, and grow through mentorship, feedback, and hands-on delivery.

What you'll do

  • • Design end-to-end flows for new and existing product features.
  • • Partner with research to validate ideas through usability testing.
  • • Contribute to and maintain our shared design system.
  • • Present work in critique and iterate based on feedback.
